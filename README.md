# 🧾 Receipt Splitter

### AI-Powered Receipt Scanner & Smart Bill Splitter

[🚀 Live Demo](https://receiptsplitter-jdx7hmrxn8q56vdewyjmmm.streamlit.app/)

Receipt Splitter is an AI-powered web application that uses Google Gemini to scan receipt images, extract items and prices, and automatically split the bill among multiple people.

## 📸 Screenshot

![Receipt Splitter Screenshot](screenshot.png)

## ✨ Features

- 📷 Upload a receipt image
- 🤖 AI-powered receipt scanning using Google Gemini
- 🧾 Extract store name, date, items, quantities, prices, tax, discount and total
- 👥 Add multiple people to the bill
- ☑️ Select who shared each item
- 💰 Automatically calculate each person's share
- 📊 Distribute tax and discounts proportionally
- ✅ Verify the calculated amount against the receipt total
- 💬 Generate a friendly WhatsApp message
- 📲 Share the final bill through WhatsApp

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Upload a receipt.
2. Gemini AI analyzes the receipt.
3. The app extracts the items, prices and bill details.
4. Add the people sharing the bill.
5. Select who shared each item.
6. The app calculates everyone's share.
7. Tax and discounts are distributed automatically.
8. Verify the calculated total.
9. Generate a WhatsApp message with the final split.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Python
- Streamlit
- Google Gemini
- Google GenAI SDK
- WhatsApp sharing

## 🎯 Purpose

Receipt Splitter makes splitting restaurant, shopping and group bills faster and easier by combining AI-powered receipt understanding with automatic bill calculations.
